StepCue setup

Let's set up StepCue on your phone

It takes about 5 minutes. We will go one step at a time, and you can stop at any point.

A family member or carer is welcome to help. Every step has a note for them.

Checking the connection

Looking for StepCue…This takes a few seconds.
For the helper Make sure this phone is on the same Wi-Fi as the StepCue laptop, and that companion_server.py is running on it. On a school or office network, turn on the laptop's Mobile hotspot and join it from this phone, then scan the new QR code from the dashboard's Access page.

Do you wear an Apple Watch?

If you do, StepCue can share your heart rate and steps with your care team. It uses Apple's free Shortcuts app.

Add the StepCue shortcut

Tap the green button. When the Shortcuts app opens, tap Add Shortcut. This page carries on by itself when you come back.

Get the shortcut
For the helper This StepCue setup does not have a shortcut link yet. Build the "StepCue" shortcut once using SHORTCUT.md in the project, share it as an iCloud link, and put that link in the laptop's .env file as STEPCUE_SHORTCUT_URL. Then restart companion_server.py.

Connect your watch

Tap Connect. If your phone asks for permission, tap Allow. It comes back here by itself.

Waiting for your heart rate…Usually under a minute.
Connect

Put StepCue on your Home Screen

Then it opens with one tap, like any other app.

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen (scroll down if needed).
  3. Tap Add at the top-right.
✓
Already on your Home Screen

A helper can do this later. It is optional.

All set

In StepCue you can tap I'm OK or I need help at any time. After a freeze it will ask if you froze, so your care team knows it got it right.
