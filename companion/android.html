StepCue for Android

Two taps to install, one tap to connect. Your phone then helps with walking and keeps your care team aware.

This page needs the code from the laptop. Scan the Android QR code on the StepCue laptop again.
Open this page on an Android phone. (iPhone? Use the iPhone QR code on the laptop.)

Already have StepCue?

Connect to this laptop

The app remembers every laptop it has connected to. New phone? Start at step 1.

1Get the app

Download StepCue

Small download (under 1 MB). It comes from your StepCue laptop, not the internet.

2Install it

  1. Tap the download when it finishes (or open Downloads).
  2. If the phone asks, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect asks, tap Install anyway.

3Connect to your laptop

Open StepCue and connect Didn't open? Tap here

Then tap Start in the app and Allow on each question. That's all.

StepCue helps with walking cues and keeps your care team aware. It does not diagnose or give medical advice. Your location stays on the StepCue laptop.
